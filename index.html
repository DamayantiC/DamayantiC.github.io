About

Where security and AI meet

I'm an MS student in Information Technology – Information Security at Carnegie Mellon University (graduating December 2026), working on both sides of AI and security: securing AI systems, and using AI to strengthen security operations.

Team project · Ethical Penetration Testing · October 2026

FSociety Cybersecurity Lab

A three-machine penetration testing lab built on TopoMojo with teammates Kedwin Chen, Keshav Ravichandra Raju, and Emily Wan, simulating a corporate network for a fictional company. Attackers scan, exploit, escalate, and pivot across machines of increasing difficulty.

My build: Level 2, a Backdrop CMS machine that serves as the pivot point to the internal network.

  • Configured a LAMP stack and deployed Backdrop CMS on a non-standard port, with two deliberate misconfigurations forming the level's attack chain.
  • Wrote a custom Python exploit automating an authenticated remote-code-execution vulnerability (CVE-2024-55655) in Backdrop CMS's module installer.
  • Designed a privilege-escalation path around a world-writable system file, paired with a simulated monitoring job to teach why detection without remediation isn't enough.
  • Built auto-graded checkpoints with hashed answer verification, and a full student walkthrough with troubleshooting notes.
  • Debugged infrastructure issues under the hood: disk space exhaustion, crashed services after accidental log deletion, and brute-force protections that needed reconfiguring for the exercise.
